I decided to leave Shopify after almost a decade.
It was hard to say goodbye, but I felt ready to take a break.
Last Friday was my last day.

I got to work on interesting problems at a scale I hadn’t imagined before joining.
I got to do it with some of the smartest, kindest people I know.
I’ll miss working with them.

When I interviewed for the Online Store team 10 years ago, they’d just added drag-and-drop support for homepage sections in the editor.
I went on to help migrate the editor to a React and GraphQL frontend and bring sections to all pages when we shipped Online Store 2.0 five years ago.

I’m glad I got to wrap up my time there by helping ship Canvas last month.
It’s a huge change from the editor I started working on -
merchants can describe what they want to change and watch AI make the edits.

As for what’s next, I’m not going to worry about that for the next 2-3 months.
I’m spending some time in India, visiting family, traveling, and writing code for fun.
The future can wait.
